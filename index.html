Enterprise administration

Your Institution. Your Rules.

Control exactly which users can access each module and what actions they are permitted to perform — with a powerful Super Administrator role and granular role-based access control.

In plain terms: each staff member sees what their job requires. Admissions officers, teachers, registrars and executives can share one platform without sharing the same level of access.

Super Admin can control

Users, roles, permissions, modules and access rights — including the ability to enable or disable functionality for your institution.

Security

Institutional data deserves disciplined access

SchoolHub is engineered around authentication, role-based permissions, controlled cloud deployment and audit-friendly administration — without overstating certifications we do not hold.

Secure user authentication

Controlled sign-in for institutional users and roles.

Role-based access

Access follows the role, not informal credential sharing.

Permission controls

Module and action-level permissions for sensitive operations.

Secure cloud deployment

Hosted with controlled access to institutional environments.

Controlled data access

Institutional information is limited to authorised users.

Database security

Protected data stores with disciplined access practices.

Audit-friendly administration

Administration patterns that support oversight.

Backups where configured

Automated database backups and recovery procedures where configured.

Deployments can be configured with POPIA-conscious data-handling practices for South Africa and GDPR-conscious practices for European institutions. This describes engineering posture — not a formal legal certification claim.
