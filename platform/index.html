Platform

One platform for institutional operations

SchoolHub is a School, College & Education Management Platform — admissions, academics, attendance, communication, finance roadmap, portals and administration working together. Broader than an LMS.

Admissions, academics, finance and communication — managed from one platform

Replace disconnected tools with one operating system for education institutions.

  • Schools and colleges on one platform
  • Online applications and enrolment
  • Granular Super Admin permissions
  • Student, parent and teacher / lecturer portals
  • Multi-campus architecture for institution groups
  • Finance, fees and payroll modules
  • Custom modules and integrations via Cyber Developers
  • International academic configuration
  • SLA-backed support from Cyber Developers

Major product areas

Explore the platform by capability — then open features for full detail and status labels.

01

Admissions & Student Management

Online applications, enrolment, learner profiles, transfers, promotion and student cards.

02

Academics & Timetables

Years, terms or semesters, classes, subjects or modules, timetables, assignments and reports.

03

Attendance & Reporting

Daily and class registers with history, absence tracking and attendance reporting.

04

Students, Parents & Staff Portals

Role-based portals for learners, guardians and teachers / lecturers.

05

Communication

Central messaging across administration, staff, students and parents.

06

Finance, Fees & Payroll

Fees, finance and HR/payroll modules — delivered according to your institution’s roadmap.

07

Campus Administration

Institution configuration, users, roles, permissions and Super Admin controls.

08

Cloud, Security & Integrations

Browser-based access, controlled permissions, backups and an integration roadmap including SA-SAMS.
